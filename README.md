# data_cleaning_activity_python_pandas
Group activity to work with a very messy and broken data base, to clean the data in order to perform a data analysis and to answer to business questions. 


## Objectives:
1. Identifying issues with 4 data frames and understanding their relations

2. Cleaning data based on the issues discovered:
 - Removing duplicates
 - Fixing or excluding incoherent or broken data
 - Identifying Null values and fixing/excluding those data
 - Identifying outliers and fixing/excluding those data
 - Ensuring data integrity with appropriate data types (Strings, Integers/Floats, Date and Time)

3. Review Business questions:
  - How should products be classified into different categories to simplify reports and analysis?
  - What is the distribution of product prices across different categories?
  - How many products are being discounted?
  - How big are the offered discounts as a percentage of the product prices?
  - How do seasonality and special dates (Christmas, Black Friday) affect sales?
  - How could data collection be improved?

4. Merge Data Frames and start Analysis
  - Creating categories based on keywords in names and description, type of product and brand names
    -  Using those product categories to understand selling volumes, revenues and discounts
  - Grouping products based on their prices
  - Identifying seasonal trends

5. Creating a presentation with the group members, dividing the workload and creating slides
